Magma Mia!

Level harness — back to the game

Arrow keys or WASD to move, Z to undo, R to restart, [ and ] for the previous and next level, K, M and X for keep, maybe and reject. Or click a cell next to the player.

How this works

A round (experiments/rounds/<name>/, made by experiments/round.js) has dry puzzles d1, d2, … ranked by score and, where a robe retrofit passed the filters, a robe version r1, r2, … with the same number as its dry puzzle. Link to one with ?round=<name>&id=d7 (add &view=d or r to limit what Prev and Next step through). Needs the local server: rounds are found through its file listing.

Verdicts are remembered in this browser while you work, and matched on the level's content, not its id. Export verdicts downloads verdicts.json; put it in the round's directory and it is loaded for anyone opening the round. The older ?src=<file-name substring> mode still reads loose files from experiments/results/ (ids <seed>.<rank>).
