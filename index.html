Magma Mia!

  • Seize the crown!
  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, the buttons below, or by tapping a square next to you.
  • Walk into a stack of blocks to push it over. It topples away from you and drops one block on each of the next squares in a line, as many as the stack was tall.
  • A block dropped on lava makes it one shallower; lava with nothing left is safe floor. Blocks that fall off the edge are lost, and blocks that hit a wall pile up against it.
  • A darker, wet-sand block is waterlogged mud: dropped on lava it fills it completely, however deep (but not the infinite lava around the edge). Touching a waterlogged block waterlogs the rest of that stack too.
  • Blocks can land on the crown and cover it. Push them off to uncover it.
  • A small purple flask on the floor is a potion: pick it up by walking onto it. It buys exactly one otherwise-fatal step onto lava, then it's used up -- so the step right after that one still has to land somewhere safe. It doesn't work on the swirling abyss around the edge, which is always fatal. While you're carrying a potion, the ring around the player turns green.
  • Stepping into lava, or off the edge, is fatal: the level restarts. There is no limit on undo.
