Levels

Magma Mia!

  • Seize the crown!
  • Walk with the arrow keys or WASD, the buttons below, or by tapping a square next to you.
  • Walk into a stack of blocks to push it over. It topples away from you and drops one block on each of the next squares in a line, as many as the stack was tall.
  • A block dropped on magma makes it one shallower; magma with nothing left is safe floor. Blocks that fall off the edge are lost, and blocks that hit a wall pile up against it.
  • Blocks can land on the crown and cover it. Push them off to uncover it.
  • Stepping into magma, or off the edge, is fatal: the level restarts. There is no limit on undo.
